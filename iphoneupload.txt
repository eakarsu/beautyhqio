 Complete Guide: Publishing iOS App to Apple App Store

  Prerequisites Checklist

  | Requirement                        | Status           |
  |------------------------------------|------------------|
  | Apple Developer Account ($99/year) | Required         |
  | Xcode installed                    | ✅ You have it   |
  | App icons (all sizes)              | Need to verify   |
  | App builds successfully            | ✅ Confirmed     |
  | Unique Bundle ID                   | com.beautyhq.app |

  ---
  Step 1: Apple Developer Program

  1. Go to https://developer.apple.com/programs
  2. Click Enroll
  3. Sign in with your Apple ID
  4. Pay $99/year fee
  5. Wait for approval (usually 24-48 hours)

  ---
  Step 2: App Store Connect Setup

  2.1 Create App Record

  1. Go to https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
  2. Click My Apps → + → New App
  3. Fill in:
    - Platform: iOS
    - Name: BeautyHQ
    - Primary Language: English
    - Bundle ID: com.beautyhq.app
    - SKU: beautyhq-ios-001 (unique identifier)

  2.2 App Information Required

  App Name:           BeautyHQ
  Subtitle:           Salon & Spa Management
  Category:           Business
  Secondary Category: Lifestyle
  Privacy Policy URL: https://beautyhq.io/privacy
  Support URL:        https://beautyhq.io/support

  2.3 Prepare App Store Assets

  | Asset              | Size         | Notes                               |
  |--------------------|--------------|-------------------------------------|
  | App Icon           | 1024x1024 px | No transparency, no rounded corners |
  | iPhone Screenshots | 1290x2796 px | 6.7" display (iPhone 15 Pro Max)    |
  | iPhone Screenshots | 1179x2556 px | 6.1" display (iPhone 15 Pro)        |
  | iPad Screenshots   | 2048x2732 px | 12.9" iPad Pro                      |

  You need 3-10 screenshots per device size

  ---
  Step 3: Certificates & Provisioning (Xcode)

  3.1 Automatic Signing (Easiest)

  1. Open BeautyHQ.xcodeproj in Xcode
  2. Select BeautyHQ target
  3. Go to Signing & Capabilities tab
  4. Check Automatically manage signing
  5. Select your Team (your Apple Developer account)

  3.2 Verify Bundle ID

  Bundle Identifier: com.beautyhq.app

  ---
  Step 4: Prepare App for Release

  4.1 Update Version Number

  In Xcode, select the target → General tab:
  Version: 1.0.0
  Build: 1

  4.2 Set Release Configuration

  1. Product → Scheme → Edit Scheme
  2. Select Archive on the left
  3. Set Build Configuration to Release

  4.3 Verify App Icons

  ---
  Step 5: Archive & Upload

  5.1 Create Archive

  # In Xcode:
  # 1. Select "Any iOS Device (arm64)" as destination
  # 2. Product → Archive
  # 3. Wait for build to complete

  Or via command line:
  cd /Users/erolakarsu/projects/beauty-wellness-ai/BeautyHQ-iOS
  xcodebuild -scheme BeautyHQ -configuration Release -destination 'generic/platform=iOS' archive -archivePath ./build/BeautyHQ.xcarchive

  5.2 Upload to App Store Connect

  Option A: Xcode Organizer (Recommended)
  1. After archiving, Organizer window opens automatically
  2. Select your archive
  3. Click Distribute App
  4. Choose App Store Connect
  5. Click Upload
  6. Wait for processing

  Option B: Command Line
  xcodebuild -exportArchive \
    -archivePath ./build/BeautyHQ.xcarchive \
    -exportPath ./build/export \
    -exportOptionsPlist ExportOptions.plist

  ---
  Step 6: App Store Connect Submission

  6.1 Complete App Information

  1. Go to App Store Connect → Your App
  2. Fill in all sections:

  Version Information:
  What's New:     Initial release of BeautyHQ - your complete salon management solution
  Description:    BeautyHQ is the all-in-one salon and spa management platform...
  Keywords:       salon, spa, booking, appointments, beauty, wellness, scheduling
  Support URL:    https://beautyhq.io/support
  Marketing URL:  https://beautyhq.io

  App Review Information:
  Contact Name:   Your Name
  Contact Phone:  Your Phone
  Contact Email:  your@email.com
  Demo Account:   (provide test login credentials)
  Notes:          Any special instructions for reviewers

  6.2 Upload Screenshots

  - Add screenshots for each device size
  - Use iPhone 15 Pro Max simulator for best quality

  6.3 Select Build

  1. In App Store Connect, go to your app version
  2. Under Build, click +
  3. Select your uploaded build

  6.4 Submit for Review

  1. Complete all required fields (check for red exclamation marks)
  2. Click Add for Review
  3. Click Submit to App Review

  ---
  Step 7: App Review Process

  | Timeline     | Stage                 |
  |--------------|-----------------------|
  | 24-48 hours  | Initial review        |
  | 1-3 days     | Average approval time |
  | Up to 7 days | If issues found       |

  Common Rejection Reasons to Avoid:

  - ❌ Crashes or bugs
  - ❌ Incomplete features (placeholder content)
  - ❌ Missing privacy policy
  - ❌ Login required without demo account
  - ❌ Missing app icon
